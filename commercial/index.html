Food Service Hot Water

Food-Service Hot Water Systems Built for the Rush

In a restaurant, the dish machine, the sinks, and the handwashing stations all pull from the same supply, and an undersized or failing heater reveals itself as lukewarm water at the dish machine and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temp dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which usually means a booster heater paired with the main system. Our food-service work covers high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and peak-demand sizing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Hot water temperature is a critical concern for any food-service operator. Health inspectors check water temperature at handwashing sinks and sanitizing stations, and a dish machine that can't reach its rinse temperature can stop service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and flow your operation depends on, with documented service records that show a well-maintained kitchen to health inspectors and landlords. We provide detailed reports after each visit, along with the records many leases and inspectors ask for.

Your schedule comes first. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. When the work can't happen during service, we install after hours, and we keep downtime to a minimum so the kitchen keeps cooking.

Safety

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect flue draft and vent connections, verify proper combustion air, test relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged correctly, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. We pull permits and schedule inspections where required. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming emergencies. It also builds a paper trail. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and inspectors that you've done your due diligence.

Rapid Response

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Wausau Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties throughout Wausau and surrounding areas, and we schedule planned work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

Preventive Care

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Wausau Water Heater program is customized to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, sediment flushing, anode rod checks, descaling for tankless units, relief valve testing, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and temperature readings at the fixtures.
